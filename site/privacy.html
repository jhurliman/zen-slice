Chord Cut — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

Chord Cut does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

How the game works

Chord Cut is fully self-contained. Every fruit, sound, and visual is generated on your device, and the game makes no network requests while you play. Your settings and best streak are stored locally on your device and never leave it.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple's Game Center, Chord Cut submits your best streak to a Game Center leaderboard. This is handled entirely by Apple through your Apple account; Chord Cut has no servers and never receives, stores, or processes your Game Center identity or any other information about you. Apple's handling of Game Center data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy. You can disable Game Center for Chord Cut in your device's Settings.

Children

Chord Cut does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
